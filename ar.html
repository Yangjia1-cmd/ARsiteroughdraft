Spawn & Grow
— AR Lens

Opens your camera and pins the live site into the space around you. Move your phone to look around. The site stays where you put it, and you can still draw on it.

  • Pin here: moves the window you're looking at to the crosshair
  • + Window: adds another copy wherever you point
  • Blend: hides the black background so only the type floats in the room
  • Landscape works best for using the controls
